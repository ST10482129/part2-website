OUR HISTORY

Growing with our community

Khula Community Bakery was established in 2023
by a local entrepreneur with a vision of creating
a bakery that could provide affordable and fresh
baked products to the community.

The bakery also aims to create part-time
employment opportunities for young people while
contributing positively to the local community.

Our product range includes bread, muffins,
cupcakes, celebration cakes, savoury pastries
and small catering packages.
